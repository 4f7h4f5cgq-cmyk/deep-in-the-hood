Throwback Thursday, Bro's turn. 1969. Isaac Hayes was a Stax songwriter, the man behind hits for Sam & Dave. Then he dropped Hot Buttered Soul, long songs, deep voice, and it sold over a million copies. Stax's biggest album to that point. 1972. His Theme from Shaft wins the Oscar for Best Original Song. He was the third Black person ever to win a competitive Oscar, after Hattie McDaniel and Sidney Poitier. Two years later he was on screen himself as Truck Turner. Range, family.
