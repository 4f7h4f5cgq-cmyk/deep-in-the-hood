FacebookReveal

Yesterday's question: name a Smokey Robinson or Miracles song. Everybody said The Tracks of My Tears, Ooo Baby Baby and Cruisin'. Respect. The slick ones went Shop Around and You've Really Got a Hold on Me. Grandma's Favorite? Quiet Storm. It's the title track of Smokey's 1975 album A Quiet Storm, and it gave a whole radio format its name. In 1976 Melvin Lindsey, a Howard student, started a late-night slow jam show at WHUR in D.C., and Cathy Hughes picked Smokey's song as the theme. Late-night R&B radio still runs on it. Who said it? Be honest. 🥧
