Throwback Thursday. Bro got the floor. 1962. Diahann Carroll. She starred on Broadway in No Strings and became the first Black woman to win the Tony for Best Actress in a Musical. 1968. She came back on NBC as Julia, a widowed nurse raising her son. It was one of the first TV shows to star a Black woman in a role that wasn't a stereotype, and it won her a Golden Globe. Then in the 80s she walked onto Dynasty and out-shaded everybody in the room. Grandma still talks about those hats. What's your favorite Diahann Carroll role? Tell us below.
