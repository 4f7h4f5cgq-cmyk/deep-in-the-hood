FacebookReveal

Yesterday's question: name a famous Black person with the last name Robinson. Everybody said Jackie Robinson, Smokey Robinson and Sugar Ray Robinson. Respect. The slick ones went Rachel Robinson and Craig Robinson. Grandma's Favorite? Sylvia Robinson. She sang "Love Is Strange" with Mickey & Sylvia in the '50s and had a solo hit with "Pillow Talk" in 1973. Then in 1979 she and her husband Joe put out "Rapper's Delight" on Sugar Hill Records, the record that took hip-hop off the block and onto the radio. They call her the Mother of Hip-Hop for a reason. Who said it? Be honest. 🥧

Yesterday's question: name a 1990s TV show with a Black lead or star. Everybody said Martin, Living Single and The Fresh Prince of Bel-Air. Respect. The slick ones went In Living Color and Hangin' with Mr. Cooper. Grandma's Favorite? Townsend Television. Robert Townsend got his own sketch and variety hour on Fox in the fall of 1993, with John Witherspoon and Paula Jai Parker in the cast and guests like Gladys Knight and James Brown. It only ran from September to December, but it was a real variety show on prime time. Who said it? Be honest. 🥧
