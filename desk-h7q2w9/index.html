FacebookThrowback thread

Throwback Thursday. November 26, 1883. Sojourner Truth passed in Battle Creek, Michigan. She was born enslaved in Ulster County, New York, as Isabella. In 1826 she walked off to freedom carrying her baby daughter. Two years later her young son Peter was illegally sold to Alabama, so she took it to court. In 1828 she won him back, one of the first Black women to beat a white man in an American court. In 1843 she renamed herself Sojourner Truth and spent the next forty years preaching abolition and women's rights across the country. Bro says: put some respect on that name.
