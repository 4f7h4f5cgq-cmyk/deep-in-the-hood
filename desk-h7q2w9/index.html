Throwback Thursday 🏠 Summer 1969. While everybody talked about Woodstock, Harlem threw six free Sunday concerts in Mount Morris Park, and close to 300,000 people came through. The lineup? Stevie Wonder. Nina Simone. Sly and the Family Stone. Mahalia Jackson. Gladys Knight & the Pips. B.B. King. One park. Free. A cameraman named Hal Tulchin filmed all of it, couldn't sell the footage, and it sat in his basement for decades. Then Questlove turned it into Summer of Soul, and it won the Oscar for Best Documentary Feature. The Harlem Cultural Festival was yesterday's Grandma's Favorite. Who had it? Be honest.
