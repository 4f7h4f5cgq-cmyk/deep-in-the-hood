Yesterday's question: name a Black woman chef. Everybody said Leah Chase, Carla Hall and Edna Lewis. Respect, Queen of Creole Cuisine got the crown. The slick ones went Nyesha Arrington and Tanya Holland. Grandma's Favorite? Lena Richard. In 1939 she put out her own cookbook in New Orleans, and a big publisher reissued it a year later. Then in October 1949 she got her own cooking show on WDSU, the city's first TV station, years before most folks had a TV in the house. Who said it? Be honest. 🥧

The slick ones went Sean Elliott and Wemby. Grandma's Favorite? James Silas.
