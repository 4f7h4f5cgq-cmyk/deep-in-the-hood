FacebookReveal

Yesterday's question: name an NFL player who went to an HBCU. Everybody said Jerry Rice, Walter Payton and Steve McNair. Respect. The slick ones went Shannon Sharpe and Deacon Jones. Grandma's Favorite? Tank Younger. Eddie Robinson's first star at Grambling. Nobody drafted him in 1949, so the Rams signed him, and he became the first HBCU player in the NFL. He won a title with them in 1951, then became the league's first Black assistant general manager in 1975. Who said it? Be honest. 🥧
